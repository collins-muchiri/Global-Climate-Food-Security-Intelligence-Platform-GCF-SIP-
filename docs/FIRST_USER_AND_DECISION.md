# The first user and the first decision

An intelligence platform without a named consumer accumulates modules and never accumulates impact.
This document names both, and is a precondition for further breadth.

First intended user: [named role at a named institution, for example a food-security analyst at a
national early-warning unit, or a programme officer at a named UN agency or NGO].

First decision the platform informs: [the specific recurring decision, for example the quarterly
early-warning classification of counties for anticipatory action, or the allocation of a contingency
budget line].

What the user currently does instead: [the manual process today, with its duration and its failure
mode]. This is the baseline the platform must beat, and the number quoted in every future outcome
statement.

Commitment sought: one hour of the user's time per month during the slice phase, in exchange for
the slice output being built to their template. A platform co-shaped by its first user is the only
kind that survives its second.
